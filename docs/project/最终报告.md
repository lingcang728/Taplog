### 1.2 Research Questions

> [!TIP]
> 和 [项目总计划](项目总计划.md) 第一节保持一致。最后一句话交代后面各章讲什么即可，不单独设小节。

- **RQ1 (understanding):** Compared with text notes, do interactive diagram notes help readers describe the relationships between concepts in a talk more accurately?
- **RQ2 (replay):** When the diagram hides details, does audio replay let readers recover the exact words and details they need, and at what cost in time and effort?
- **RQ3 (repeated points):** When the diagram marks that a point is covered again later in the talk, do readers listen to it, and can they report what was added?
- **Exploratory question (live Q&A):** While recording a talk, what do users ask, and do the answers help without interrupting listening?
